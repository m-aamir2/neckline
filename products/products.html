Frequently asked questions

How does the Neckline® 4-in-1 massager work?

Neckline® helps with neck pain by combining stretching, massage, EMS and heat. A special 26° cervical traction relaxes the muscles, while EMS improves blood circulation. The massage cushions release tension, and heat dilates the blood vessels, which promotes healing. Just 10–15 minutes a day is enough to achieve noticeable relief – often from the first day.

When will I notice an improvement?

About 10-15 minutes of daily use – that’s how long it takes for the discomfort to start to subside. Within 1-2 weeks, 92% of our customers report that their neck pain is completely gone. The most important thing is consistent use to address the real cause of the pain and provide lasting relief.

What pains can Neckline® eliminate?

Neckline® is ideal for treating neck, shoulder and upper back pain, hunchback, forward head posture, pinched nerves in the neck, temporomandibular joint (TMJ) pain, migraines and tension headaches.

Can Neckline® be used in bed?

Yes, the Neckline® massager can also be used in bed – the massage, heat, EMS and acupressure functions work without any problems. However, the stretching function depends on a firm surface. If lying on the floor is uncomfortable for you, you can use a blanket to make the surface a little softer. This way, the stretching function still remains fully effective.

Is Neckline® a long-term solution?

That's why we developed Neckline®. We discovered that many products on the market only provide short-term relief but no lasting solution. The Neckline® massager has been specifically developed to provide long-term correction of neck pain, tension, headaches, neck misalignment and the so-called mobile neck – for a healthy and upright posture, for life.

How to use Neckline®?

1. Press the power button to start Neckline®.

2. Lie down on the massage machine and adjust the temperature, massage and EMS levels to the level that is most comfortable for you.

3. Relax for 10-15 minutes while the massage takes effect.

4. Repeat this daily for 7-14 days and then as needed (whenever you feel tension or pain in your neck).

What if the Neckline® 4-in-1 Massager doesn't work for me?

We want you to be able to try our massager completely risk-free. That's why we offer you our 60-day pain-free guarantee: You have 60 days to try the device in peace and quiet. If during this time you notice that the massager does not meet your expectations, you can return it to us. In that case, we will refund you the full purchase price.
